PAYPAL DEVELOPER PLATFORM · SANDBOX

PayPal Checkout

Test the fiat checkout and buyer approval flow with PayPal Sandbox. This is a sandbox payment, not an on-chain PYUSD transfer.

Sandbox credentials are required on the server. After buyer approval, the return flow verifies the order and captures it. Webhook signatures are verified separately.

SOLANA MAINNET · TOKEN-2022

Pay with PYUSD

Connect a Solana wallet holding authentic PYUSD. The app prepares a transfer; your wallet displays the transaction and you approve it yourself.

Requires PYUSD and SOL for network fees. Mainnet transaction is irreversible. Check the recipient address in your wallet before approving.

VERCEL AI GATEWAY · HUMAN APPROVAL REQUIRED

Ask the swarm to route an intent

The AI reads your task and proposes the best available agent. It cannot move funds, sign a transaction, trade, settle a channel or capture a PayPal order.

The token is used only for this request and is not saved by this page. Configure the AI model and Gateway credentials server-side.

Important: PYUSD is a USD stablecoin issued by Paxos and branded by PayPal, not a PayPal fiat balance sitting directly on Solana. PayPal Sandbox checkout does not automatically convert or bridge funds into PYUSD. The Mainnet rail only transfers PYUSD already held by the connected wallet. Paxos retains active issuer controls (permanent delegate and freeze authority), so PYUSD is not censorship-resistant.
